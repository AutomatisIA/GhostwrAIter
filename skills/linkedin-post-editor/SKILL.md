# linkedin-post-editor

## Purpose

Corriger et renforcer un draft pour le rendre plus net, plus credible et mieux aligne au positionnement.

## Inputs

- `draft` (headline + bodyMarkdown)
- `currentQualityScore` (0.0 to 1.0)
- `ideaTitle`, `ideaAngle` (the original idea)
- `typology`, `objective` (the editorial intent)
- `structureKey`, `structureLabel` (the chosen narrative structure)
- `selectedHookText` (the hook the user chose)
- `context.voiceRules` (full list of editorial rules)

## Outputs

- draft corrige
- score qualite (must be higher than currentQualityScore or explain why not)

## Prompt

You receive a LinkedIn draft that was already generated by the post-writer skill. Your job is to make it significantly better, not to polish it.

BEFORE rewriting, diagnose what is weak. Check each of these against the provided context:
1. Does the opening line actually stop the scroll, or is it a soft warm-up?
2. Does the argument connect to the reader's specific pain (from context.voiceRules, ICP data, and the ideaAngle)?
3. Is the structure (structureLabel) actually respected in the body, or does it drift?
4. Does the post deliver on the objective (awareness = insight, conversion = CTA, engagement = debate trigger)?
5. Are there generic phrases that could appear in any post? Remove them.
6. Is the conclusion sharp and specific to an SME decision-maker, or does it land softly?
7. Does the draft respect ALL voice rules in context.voiceRules, not just the anti-style ones?

THEN rewrite. The rewrite must:
- Fix every diagnosed weakness
- Preserve the core idea and the chosen hook
- Tighten the argument (cut filler, merge redundant paragraphs)
- Add at least one concrete operational detail if the original is too abstract
- Make the conclusion actionable when the objective is "conversion"
- Aim for a qualityScore strictly above the currentQualityScore

Do not append editorial commentary, correction notes, or meta-writing language.
Do not add "Version revue" or "Ce post part d'un constat" type phrases.

Return this exact success shape: {"status":"succeeded","summary":"...","data":{"draft":{"headline":"...","bodyMarkdown":"..."},"qualitySignals":{"clarity":0.0,"specificity":0.0,"antiHypeAlignment":0.0}},"error":null}

If the draft is already strong and you cannot improve it meaningfully, return it unchanged with the same qualitySignals. Do not degrade a good draft.
